Restbell

Rest, rounds and intervals. iPhone and Apple Watch.

A timer for the two gaps in a training session — the rest between your lifting sets, and the work and rest blocks of interval training. One object does both, so a push day and a Tabata session live in the same app instead of two.

What free means here

  • Every mode. Unlimited runs. The timer is never locked.
  • Five saved routines of your own, with the presets on top.
  • The full Apple Watch app — standalone, no phone needed.
  • All the cues, and export whenever you like.

No ads. No account. No network.

Restbell makes no network calls at all. Nothing to sign up for, nothing tracked, nothing that leaves your phone.
