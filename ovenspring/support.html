Sourdough starter tracker for iPhone

Measuring a rise

Photograph the jar right after feeding, then drag the two lines: one on the jar floor, one on the level of the culture. When it has domed, photograph it again and mark the new level. Ovenspring works out the percentage and the time it took. The measurement is arithmetic on your own marks — the app never guesses a line for you.

The verdict

Feed today, fridge it, or bake-ready. It is computed from how regularly you have fed the starter and how it has actually risen, so it sharpens as the record grows. A brand-new starter shows a "still learning" state rather than a confident number.

Reminders

Each starter has its own cadence — daily on the counter, or every few days in the fridge. Bake plans schedule a notification for every step. All reminders can be cleared at once in Settings.

Ovenspring Pro

Logging is free forever: one starter, unlimited feedings, the full rise measurement, the verdict and the calculator never cost anything. Pro adds unlimited starters, full history and insights, the bake timeline planner, and export. It is a monthly or yearly subscription, with a 7-day free trial on the yearly plan. Manage or cancel in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.

Your data

Everything lives on your iPhone — no account, no cloud, no analytics. Export writes your whole record to a file you keep. See the privacy policy.
