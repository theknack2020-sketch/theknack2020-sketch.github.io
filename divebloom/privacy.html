Divebloom — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Nothing reaches us

Divebloom does not collect any personal data, and we run no server for the game. There is no account of ours, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDK. The game makes no network requests of its own. The only game data that leaves your device goes to two Apple services you control, iCloud and Game Center, described below.

What stays on your device

Your lives and tools, and your sound, music and haptics settings, are stored on your device only. Deleting the app deletes them.

iCloud

When you are signed in to iCloud, the levels you have reached, your stars and best scores, your logbook (streak, Daily Dive results, stamps and Free Dive record), your Field Journal and your trophies are also kept in your own iCloud through Apple's key-value storage, so another device signed in to the same Apple Account can continue the dive. That copy lives in your iCloud account and we cannot read it. Without an iCloud account these stay on your device only.

Game Center

Game Center is optional. The game uses it only when your device is already signed in to Game Center or you sign in from the game's Settings; nothing in the game waits for it. Then your achievements and your deepest Free Dive are reported to Apple's Game Center, where your Game Center friends may see them, under Apple's privacy policy. We receive nothing from it.

Purchases

The Dive Pass and the supply packs are sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment and holds the receipt; we receive no name, address or card details. The game checks your purchases with Apple's StoreKit on the device.

If you write to us

If you email us, from this site or from "Tell us what to fix" in the game, we receive your email address and what you write, and use them only to answer you. Ask and we delete them.

Children

Divebloom collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at this address with a new date.
