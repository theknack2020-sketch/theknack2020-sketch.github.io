Divebloom — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Nothing leaves your phone

Divebloom does not collect, transmit or store any personal data on any server. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDK. The game makes no network requests of its own.

What stays on your device

The levels you have reached, your stars and best scores, your lives and boosters, and your sound and haptics settings are stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

The Dive Pass is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment and holds the receipt; we receive no name, address or card details. The game checks your subscription with Apple's StoreKit on the device.

Children

Divebloom collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at this address with a new date.
