Divebloom — Terms of Use

Last updated: 30 September 2026

What Divebloom is

Divebloom is a match-three puzzle game: 1,000 levels laid on a dive from the surface to the Challenger Deep, voyages that go on past them, a Daily Dive and a Free Dive. It plays offline on your device and needs no account.

What is free

Every level is free for everyone. You have five lives, and one comes back every 30 minutes; a life is used when you start playing a level and returned when you win it. Tools are earned in play: two each of the Scoop, Whirlpool and Air Tank to start, two of every other tool when you reach it, a new set every tenth level you clear for the first time, and more from chests, stamp cards and events.

Dive Pass

The Dive Pass is an auto-renewable subscription sold through Apple, with a weekly plan and a monthly plan and no free trial. While it is active you have unlimited lives and receive a Scoop, a Whirlpool and an Air Tank each calendar day, plus that weekday's tool once it is in your kit.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. The subscription renews automatically at the price shown unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends. You can manage or cancel it in iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Prices are shown in the app in your local currency, from the App Store.

Supply packs

Two supply packs are sold through Apple as one-time purchases: 3 Air Tanks, and the Tool Chest (six Scoops, six Whirlpools and six Air Tanks). Their contents are fixed and never expire. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. A pack's tools go into the kit on the device where you bought it; like other one-time purchases of this kind, packs are not restored on another device or after the app is deleted. Refunds are handled by Apple.

Lives and tools

Lives and tools are part of the game. They have no cash value, cannot be exchanged or transferred, and are kept on the device where they were earned or bought.

Your data

Your progress is kept on your device and, when you are signed in to iCloud, in your own iCloud so your other devices can continue the dive. Game Center is optional. See the Privacy Policy.
