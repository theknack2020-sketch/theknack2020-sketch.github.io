Divebloom — Terms of Use

Last updated: 28 September 2026

What Divebloom is

Divebloom is a match-three puzzle game of 1,000 levels laid on a dive from the surface to the Challenger Deep. It plays offline on your device and needs no account.

What is free

Every level is free for everyone. You have five lives, and one comes back every 30 minutes; a life is used when you start playing a level and returned when you win it. Boosters (Scoop, Whirlpool, Air Tank) are earned in play: two of each to start, and a new set every tenth level you clear for the first time.

Dive Pass

The Dive Pass is an auto-renewable subscription sold through Apple, with a weekly plan and a monthly plan and no free trial. While it is active you have unlimited lives and receive one Scoop, one Whirlpool and one Air Tank each calendar day.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. The subscription renews automatically at the price shown unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends. You can manage or cancel it in iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Prices are shown in the app in your local currency, from the App Store.

Lives and boosters

Lives and boosters are part of the game. They have no cash value, cannot be exchanged or transferred, and are kept on your device.

Your data

Your progress and settings are stored on your device. See the Privacy Policy.
