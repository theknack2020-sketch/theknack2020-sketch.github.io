Common questions

I am out of lives. One life comes back every 30 minutes, up to five. Winning a level returns the life it used. The Dive Pass gives unlimited lives.

A sea mine went off. A mine counts down with every move; clear it by matching it or catching it in a blast before it reaches zero. An Air Tank continues the dive with five more moves and gives every mine five more moves too.

I bought the Dive Pass on another device. Open Settings in the game and choose Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple Account.

My progress is gone after reinstalling. Progress is stored only on your device, so deleting the app deletes it. There is no account or cloud copy.

No sound. Check the silent switch and the sound setting in the game's Settings.
