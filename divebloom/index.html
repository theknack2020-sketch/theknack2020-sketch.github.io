Divebloom

A match-three dive from the sunlit reef to the Challenger Deep, 10,935 metres down. One thousand levels, each a few metres deeper than the last. The light fades as you go, and below 1,000 metres the creatures light the board themselves.

How it plays

Swap neighbouring sea creatures to line up three or more. Bigger matches make Surges, Bubbles, Mantas, Compasses and Pearls. Break rocks, cut nets, open treasure chests and clear sea mines before they reach zero. Every 50th level the Kraken rises from the dark.

Every day in the sea

A Daily Dive with the same board for everyone on the same day, a sea calendar that follows the real moon, glow nights and spawning nights, a visiting creature each month, a weekly expedition, messages in bottles, treasure maps and a Field Journal of 40 real species. In Free Dive you take one breath and go as deep as you can.

Past the bottom

At the Challenger Deep the voyages begin, and the dive carries on through the world's other trenches.

Fair by design

Every level can be played without paying. No account, no ads, no tracking, and the game plays offline. Your progress follows you through your own iCloud; Game Center is optional.
