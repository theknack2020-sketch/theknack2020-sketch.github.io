Divebloom

A match-three dive from the sunlit reef to the Challenger Deep, 10,935 metres down. One thousand levels, each a few metres deeper than the last. The light fades as you go, and below 1,000 metres the creatures light the board themselves.

Swap neighbouring sea creatures to line up three or more. Bigger matches make Surges, Bubbles, Mantas, Compasses and Pearls. Break rocks, cut nets, open treasure chests and clear sea mines before they reach zero.

No account, no ads, no tracking. The game plays offline.
