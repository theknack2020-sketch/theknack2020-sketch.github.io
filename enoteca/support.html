Bringing a cellar you already keep

Export a CSV from wherever your cellar lives now, then open Enoteca → Settings → Import and choose the file.

  • Column order does not matter. Enoteca reads the header and recognises common names from other apps — wine, winery, year, qty, varietal, appellation, bin and more.
  • A row with only a name still lands. Nothing is dropped in silence: the receipt at the end tells you exactly how many arrived, how many need a region, and how many rows had nothing to record.
  • Quoted fields, embedded commas and multi-line tasting notes are handled, and Windows line endings are fine.
  • Import does not upload anything. The file is read on your device.

The free tier and Enoteca Pro

Enoteca holds 200 bottles free, with notes, drinking windows, search and export all included. Enoteca Pro removes the ceiling. Nothing you have already entered is ever locked, and export stays free forever on every tier.

If an import runs into the free ceiling, the rows that did not fit are reported as waiting — your file is untouched, and importing it again after subscribing brings them in.

Managing or cancelling a subscription

Subscriptions are handled by Apple, not by us: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Enoteca. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Refund requests go to Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Restoring a purchase

Open the Enoteca Pro screen and tap Restore purchases. If it says nothing was found, the subscription is probably on a different Apple Account — sign in with that one and try again.

A grape or region is missing

The bundled list carries 1,892 grapes and 761 regions, and it will never be complete. Anything not on it can be typed in by hand — no field ever blocks you from saving a bottle. If you think something common is missing, email us and we will add it in the next update.

Fixing a mistake

Every bottle, tasting note and storage location can be edited or deleted. Tap a bottle → Edit this bottle; tap a note to edit it. Deletions ask first. A mistyped entry is never permanent.

Where your data lives

On your iPhone, and nowhere else. There is no account, no cloud sync and no server. Because of that, keep your own backups: export a CSV now and then, and keep your encrypted device backup switched on. Details in the Privacy Policy.
