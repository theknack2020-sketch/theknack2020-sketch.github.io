Enoteca

Your cellar, drawn in its own colour — and told plainly what is ready tonight.

Enoteca is a cellar book: what wine you own, where it is, when it should be drunk, and what you thought of it last time. Every wine appears as the liquid it is, and the level in the glass shows how much of what you once held is still there. Region and grape are picked from a bundled list of 1,892 grapes and 761 regions, and anything not on that list is still welcome — no field ever blocks saving. Bring a cellar you already keep as a CSV, and take yours out the same way, free on every tier. Everything stays on your device: no account, no cloud, no tracking.
