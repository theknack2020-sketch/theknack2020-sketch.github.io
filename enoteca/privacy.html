Privacy Policy

Effective: August 19, 2026 · Last updated: August 19, 2026

The short version:

Your cellar stays on your device. Every bottle, tasting note, storage location and photo you record in Enoteca is stored only on your iPhone using Apple's SwiftData, with iCloud sync deliberately switched off. There is no account and no login. We never receive any of it. Enoteca has no analytics and no tracking of any kind, and we never show ads. Export is free, forever.

1. Who we are

Enoteca ("the app", "we", "us") is developed and distributed by TheKnack. Questions or concerns: theknack2020@gmail.com.

2. Your cellar stays on your device

Enoteca is a wine cellar journal. Everything you record is stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's SwiftData, and it is kept there only:

  • Bottles — names, producers, vintages, colour, region, grape, how many you hold and how many you ever held
  • Drinking windows — the years you decide a wine is ready between
  • Tasting notes you write, with their dates and the vintage each one is about
  • Storage locations you name — racks, shelves, bins
  • Photos you choose for a bottle

We do not upload, mirror, sync, or read any of this. There is no Enoteca server. The app makes no network calls at all beyond Apple's own App Store purchase check.

3. Photos

Enoteca asks for a photo only at the moment you attach one to a bottle — never on first launch.

  • Enoteca uses Apple's own system photo picker, which runs outside the app. It receives only the single picture you choose and never sees the rest of your library. Because of this, iOS does not need to show you a library-permission prompt.
  • The picture you pick is resized and stored in the app's own local database on your device. It is not uploaded anywhere.
  • Declining is fully supported. Every other part of a bottle's record works exactly the same without a photo. Nothing is blanked out and nothing is nagged.

4. The grape and region list

Enoteca ships with a reference list of 1,892 grape varieties and 761 wine regions, bundled inside the app itself. It is read from the app's own files on your device — looking something up sends no request anywhere, and we never learn what you searched for. The list is derived from Wikidata, which is published under CC0.

5. Exporting your data

Enoteca exports your cellar as a CSV file at your request, using Apple's standard file exporter — you choose where the file goes. The file is created on your device and is never routed through us. Export is free forever and is never placed behind a subscription. A cellar you cannot take with you is not really yours, and we do not treat it as leverage.

6. Importing a cellar you already keep

If you open a CSV export from another app, Enoteca reads it directly on your device and writes the rows into your local cellar. The file is not uploaded, not copied to us, and not sent anywhere. You choose the file with Apple's own file picker, and Enoteca can read nothing else on your device.

7. Analytics: none

Enoteca contains no analytics or telemetry of any kind. There is no third-party analytics SDK, no usage tracking, and no device identifier collection — not even an anonymous "app opened" counter ever leaves your phone.

This is why Enoteca's App Store privacy label reads "Data Not Collected". Any sense of how the app is doing comes only from Apple's own aggregate App Store Connect figures, which never reach into your device.

8. What we do not do

  • We do not require an account, sign-up, or login.
  • We do not store your bottles, notes or photos on any server — there is no Enoteca server.
  • We do not put your cellar in iCloud; the app's database has CloudKit sync switched off.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers, and we never show ads.
  • We do not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt — there is nothing to track.
  • We do not send notifications; version 1.0 schedules none at all.
  • We do not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs — only Apple's own frameworks (SwiftUI, SwiftData, PhotosUI, StoreKit).

9. App Store purchases

If you subscribe to Enoteca Pro (monthly or yearly), Apple processes the transaction. We receive from Apple only a receipt confirming your entitlement status; we do not receive your payment information. Apple's privacy policy governs payment data: apple.com/legal/privacy.

10. Age

Enoteca is a record-keeping tool for wine and is rated 17+. It is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from them. Because the app collects nothing from anyone, there is no children's data for us to hold.

11. Keeping, changing and deleting your records

  • Edit: every bottle, note and location you create can be corrected in the app. A mistyped entry is never a permanent error.
  • Delete: every record can be removed, and every deletion is confirmed before it happens.
  • Delete everything: deleting the app removes all of its local data from your device. Since Enoteca keeps nothing in iCloud and nothing on a server, that is the end of it.
  • Backups: copies may persist inside encrypted device or iCloud device backups you keep, which are managed by Apple, until those backups are deleted or rotate out.

We retain no server-side copy of your content, because we never receive it.

12. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA and other regions)

Because we hold no personal data about you on any server, most data-subject requests resolve on your own device:

  • Enoteca collects nothing about you, so there is no personal data held by us to access, correct, or erase.
  • We do not "sell" or "share" personal information as those terms are defined under the CCPA/CPRA.
  • EU/EEA and UK residents retain all rights under the GDPR/UK GDPR, including access, rectification, erasure, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. California residents retain all rights under the CCPA/CPRA.
  • To exercise any right, or if you have questions, email theknack2020@gmail.com.

If we ever change how Enoteca handles data, this page will update with a new effective date.
