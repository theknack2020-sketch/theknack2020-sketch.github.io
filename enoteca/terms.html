The short version:

Enoteca is a wine cellar journal for iPhone. It is free to hold up to 200 bottles, with tasting notes, drinking windows, search and CSV export all included at no cost. Enoteca Pro is an auto-renewing subscription that removes the bottle ceiling. Your records stay on your device, and export is never placed behind the subscription.

2. What the free version includes

Without paying anything, Enoteca gives you:

  • Up to 200 bottles in your cellar
  • Unlimited tasting notes on those bottles
  • Drinking windows and the Ready view that reads them
  • Storage locations, search, and the bundled list of 1,892 grapes and 761 regions
  • Import from a CSV export of another app
  • CSV export, free forever — this is never moved behind the subscription

3. Enoteca Pro

Enoteca Pro is an auto-renewing subscription. It removes the 200-bottle ceiling, so your cellar can grow without limit. That is what it adds — the app does not hide existing features behind it, and nothing you have already entered is ever locked.

  • Monthly and yearly plans are offered. Prices are shown in the app in your local currency, taken live from the App Store.
  • A free trial may be offered to new subscribers. The app states the exact length and the first charge date on the purchase screen before you buy, and it says so only when your Apple Account is actually eligible for it.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase.
  • The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple Account settings: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
  • If a free trial is offered, any unused portion is forfeited when you purchase a subscription.

5. What happens if you stop subscribing

If a Pro subscription ends, nothing you recorded is deleted and nothing is hidden from you. Your whole cellar remains readable and exportable. Only the ability to add bottles beyond the free ceiling stops until you subscribe again.

Everything you enter — bottles, notes, locations, photos — belongs to you. It is stored on your device, we never receive it, and you can export it as a CSV file at any time, on any tier. See the Privacy Policy.

7. Reference data

The bundled grape and region list is derived from Wikidata and is published under CC0. It is provided as a convenience for typing, not as an authority: any grape or region not on the list can still be entered by hand, and no field ever blocks you from saving a bottle.

8. Wine, drinking windows and your judgement

Enoteca records what you decide about your wine. Drinking windows are the years you enter yourself; the app reads them back to you and never invents them. Nothing in the app is a professional appraisal, a valuation, or advice about whether a wine is sound to drink. Use your own judgement, and your own nose.
