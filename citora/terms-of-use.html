Citora: City Travel Log · Effective 6 August 2026

The app

Citora is a personal travel record: you mark cities you have visited, and the app renders your map, stats and share cards. Marking cities, the map, core stats, notes with up to three photos per city, and full export/import are free, forever.

Citora Pro

Citora Pro is an auto-renewing subscription (monthly or yearly; the yearly plan includes a 7-day free trial) unlocking unlimited photos per city, the Chart and Globe share-card styles, the full badge collection, advanced stats, and every widget style. Prices are shown in the app before purchase. Payment is charged to your Apple Account; the subscription renews unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel any time in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple.
