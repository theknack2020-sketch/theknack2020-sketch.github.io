Horizora

Count the days to the things that matter — on your Home Screen, free, and stored on your phone where nothing can quietly delete them.

What it does

Horizora holds the dates you are waiting for and the dates you are counting away from. A wedding, a trip, a first birthday, a lease ending — and the ones you keep rather than await: a sobriety date, an anniversary, someone you remember. They sit in one list in the order they will happen, and they go on your Home Screen.

The widget is free

Every widget size, permanently, with no trial and no unlock. It is the point of the app, so it is not the thing we sell.

Your dates stay yours

There is no account, no server and no sync. Everything lives on your iPhone. Horizora can write a backup file into your own iCloud Drive so a new phone can pick up where the old one left off — and backup and restore are free, always.

No tracking, at all

No analytics SDK, no advertising identifier, no data collection of any kind. Horizora makes no network request.

Horizora Pro

An optional subscription that adds five things: unlimited countdowns past the free ten, a photo behind a countdown, Lock Screen and StandBy widgets, one widget that rotates through several dates, and custom milestone reminders. A 7-day free trial is offered; the Terms of Use set out the renewal and cancellation terms in full.
