Common questions

Where does the stylus figure come from? It is the sum of the plays you have logged, in hours, against the rated life you set for that stylus. It is an estimate and the app says so wherever it appears. Amber means "due", not "broken".

Can I change the rated hours? Yes — Settings → Stylus. Manufacturers quote anywhere from 500 to 1,500 hours; the figure is yours to set.

Does Feltside tell me what my records are worth? No, deliberately. Feltside keeps what you own and how it is doing; it is not a price guide.

How do I get my data out? Settings → Export, as CSV or JSON. Free in every tier, always.

How do I cancel? Subscriptions are managed in your Apple Account settings.
