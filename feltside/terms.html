Feltside — Terms of Use

Last updated: 13 August 2026

The app

Feltside is a personal record-collection log published by TheKnack. It keeps what you own, its condition, what you have played and an estimate of your stylus's remaining life.

Estimates are estimates

The stylus figure is calculated from the plays you log against a rated life you set. It is not a measurement of physical wear and must not be treated as one. Cleaning suggestions are guidance, not instructions. Feltside does not estimate what any record is worth, and nothing in the app is a valuation.

Subscription

Feltside Pro is offered as a monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription. The yearly plan may include a 7-day free trial for eligible accounts: 7 days free, then the yearly price. Cancel any time in your Apple Account settings before the trial ends and nothing is charged.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation. A subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours of the end of the period. You manage and cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings. Prices are shown in the app in your local currency.

What free always includes

Up to 25 records with full detail, every play and cleaning you log, 5 wantlist items, search, and export to CSV and JSON — free in every tier, always. Reaching the free limit never affects records you have already added.

Your content

Everything you enter is yours. TheKnack claims no rights over it and has no access to it.
