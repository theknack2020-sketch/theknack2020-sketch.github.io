Feltside — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 August 2026

The short version

Feltside collects nothing. There is no analytics SDK, no tracking, no advertising identifier and no account. Your collection lives on your device.

What stays on your device

Every record, play, cleaning entry, stylus, wantlist item, photo and note you enter is stored locally on your iPhone. If you turn on iCloud backup, that data is stored in your iCloud account under Apple's terms — TheKnack has no access to it and no server that receives it.

Record lookup

If you look a record up, Feltside sends only the words you typed (or a scanned barcode) to the MusicBrainz web service, whose database is public-domain (CC0), in order to return matching release details. No identifier of you or your device is attached to that request, and results are stored only on your device. Cover images may be fetched from the Cover Art Archive in the same way.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. Feltside receives from Apple only whether a subscription is active. We never see your payment details.

Your data is portable

Export to CSV or JSON is available in every tier, free, at any time, from Settings. Deleting the app removes the local data; anything in your iCloud backup is removed with the backup.

Children

Feltside is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
