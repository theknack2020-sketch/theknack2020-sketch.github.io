What Fastlight is

Fastlight tells you whether today is a fast day in the calendar you keep — Catholic, Orthodox or Evangelical — what the rule is and what is coming; it keeps the fast you are on with a timetable of prayer hours, a journal line a day and reminders; and it carries the Daniel fast food rules and a library of prayer prompts. Everything is computed on your device from the church calendar (Western Easter and Orthodox Pascha) — nothing is downloaded and there is no account.

What is free

Today's rule, one running fast, the Daniel fast rules, twenty prompts, your last kept fast, one small Home Screen widget and the calendar seven days ahead are free, permanently, with no account.

Fastlight Pro

Fastlight Pro is an auto-renewable subscription sold through Apple: one monthly plan and one yearly plan, each with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. It adds the calendar for every year ahead in all three traditions, every fast plan (21 days, Lent's 40, an Orthodox season, a custom range), all 120 prompts, history and its figures, the medium widget and the Lock Screen accessories, and the eve-of-fast-day reminder with the verse inside each hour's reminder.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase, or at the end of the free trial if one applies. The subscription renews automatically unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends. You can manage or cancel it in iOS Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions. Prices are shown in the app in your local currency, from the App Store.

The calendar

Fast days follow the published discipline of each tradition as Fastlight understands it (Canon Law and the USCCB norms for the Catholic calendar; the common typikon practice for the Orthodox calendar; the January 21-day season by custom for the Evangelical calendar). Your parish, diocese or church may keep a different rule; theirs governs. Fastlight gives no theological, dietary or medical advice.

Health

Fasting is a religious practice, not medical advice. If you are pregnant, diabetic, under 18, or under a doctor's care, ask your doctor before fasting. Stop a fast if you feel unwell.
