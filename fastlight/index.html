Fastlight — Fast Days & Prayer

Today is a fast day, or it is not. Fastlight tells you which, in your church — Catholic, Orthodox or Evangelical — and what the rule is. It keeps the fast you are on as a timetable of prayer hours, one journal line a day, and carries the Daniel fast rules and 120 prayer prompts. Everything is computed on your phone: no account, no network, nothing leaves the device.
