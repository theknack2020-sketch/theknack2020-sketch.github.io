Common questions

Today's rule looks wrong for my church. Check the tradition in Settings › Calendar. Orthodox users can choose the Revised Julian or Julian (Old Calendar) style there. If a date still disagrees with your parish calendar, tell us the date and the rule you expected — the calendar is computed and a wrong day is a bug we fix.

Reminders do not arrive. Reminders are asked on the app's own card when you start your first fast. If you said Not now, turn them on in iOS Settings › Notifications › Fastlight. The timetable, the calendar and the widget work without them.

I bought Pro on another phone. Open Settings › Fastlight Pro › Restore purchases while signed in to the same Apple Account.

I deleted a fast by mistake. The last-action line at the foot of History (or the edit sheet) offers Undo for 10 seconds after a deletion.
