Fastlight — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

Nothing leaves your phone

Fastlight does not collect, transmit or store any personal data on any server. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDK. The app makes no network requests of its own.

What stays on your device

Your chosen tradition and calendar style, your prayer-hour times, your fasts and their journal lines, and your reminder preferences are stored on your device (and in the app's own container shared with its widgets). Deleting the app deletes them.

Notifications

If you allow reminders, Fastlight schedules local notifications on your device for the hours of a running fast and, with Pro, for the eve of a fast day. They are created and delivered by iOS on the phone; nothing is sent to us.

Purchases

Fastlight Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment and holds the receipt; we receive no name, address or card details. The app checks your subscription with Apple's StoreKit on the device.

Children

Fastlight is rated 4+ and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at this address with a new date.
