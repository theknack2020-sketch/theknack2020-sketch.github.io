Privacy Policy

Effective: July 26, 2026

The short version:

Dayrow collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics and no server of ours. The only network Dayrow touches is Apple's App Store, to sell and restore Dayrow Pro. Your child's routines stay on the device you made them on.

Dayrow collects nothing

Dayrow has no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no third-party software of any kind. It does not ask you to make an account, and there is no account to make. The only network Dayrow touches is Apple's own App Store, and only to sell and restore Dayrow Pro — Dayrow has no server of its own for your data to be sent to.

What stays on your device

Your children's names, your routines, the cards you make, the photos you choose and any voice labels you record are stored on your device and nowhere else. Nothing is uploaded to us, because there is no us to upload to.

Photos and the microphone

Dayrow asks for your photo library only when you choose a picture for a card, and for the microphone only while you are recording a voice label. Both are used for that and nothing else, and both stay on your device with the card.

Children

Dayrow is bought and set up by an adult. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children. It shows no advertising and contains no links out of your child's screen.

App Store purchases

Dayrow Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.
