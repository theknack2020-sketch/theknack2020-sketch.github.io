Privacy Policy

Effective: July 26, 2026

The short version:

Dayrow collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics and no server of ours — the app has no networking code in it at all. Your child's routines stay on your device, and in your own iCloud if you switch that on.

Dayrow collects nothing

Dayrow has no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no third-party software of any kind. It does not ask you to make an account, and there is no account to make. This is not a policy we intend to follow: the app contains no networking code, so there is nothing in it that could send your data anywhere.

What stays on your device

Your children's names, your routines, the cards you make, the photos you choose and any voice labels you record are stored on your device. Nothing is uploaded to us, because there is no us to upload to.

iCloud

If you turn on iCloud sync, your routines are copied to your own private iCloud database so your other devices can read them. That is Apple's service and your account. We cannot see the contents, and we receive nothing.

Photos and the microphone

Dayrow asks for your photo library only when you choose a picture for a card, and for the microphone only while you are recording a voice label. Both are used for that and nothing else, and both stay with the card.

Children

Dayrow is bought and set up by an adult. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children. It shows no advertising and contains no links out of your child's screen.

App Store purchases

Dayrow Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.
