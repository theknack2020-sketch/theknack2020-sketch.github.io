Dayrow for iPhone and iPad

Build your first routine

Tap Add a routine, give it a name, then Add a card for each step. Drag the cards to reorder them. Tap a card to change its wording or give it a quiet timer.

Hand the device to your child

Tap Start. Your child sees one big card for now and a small one for what is next, and nothing else. To come back, press and hold the top-left corner of the screen for three seconds and enter your passcode.

Lock the screen properly

Dayrow's own screen has no way out except your passcode. iOS Guided Access goes further and stops the Home gesture too: open the Settings app → Accessibility → Guided Access, turn it on and set a passcode there. Then, inside Dayrow, press the side button three times quickly and tap Start.

Print a strip

Open a routine and tap Print a strip. Dayrow lays the same row out on paper for the fridge, the wall or the classroom.

Make a card from your own photo

In the card picker, tap the dashed card with the camera on it. Choose a photo, write what it says, and — with Dayrow Pro — record a voice your child knows saying the step.

Restore a purchase

Settings → Restore a purchase. Dayrow Pro is a one-time purchase tied to the Apple ID that bought it.

Nothing is measured

Dayrow keeps no score, no history and no percentages of how your child did. If you are looking for that, Dayrow is deliberately not the app.
