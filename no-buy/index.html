NoBuy: No Spend Day Tracker

Version 2.1.1 from TheKnack

NoBuy: No Spend Day Tracker is an iPhone and iPad app for no-spend and no-buy challenges: each night it asks one question, “Buy anything unnecessary today?”, and counts the days you keep. For the moment you want to buy something, it has a ten-minute urge timer, a five-question impulse checklist and a waiting list that holds a thing for 24, 48 or 72 hours. There is no bank connection and no account, and everything you log stays on your device.

Who it is for

  • People doing a no-spend or no-buy challenge, from a first week to a low-buy year.
  • People who want a pause between wanting something and buying it.
  • People saving toward one thing who want an estimate of what their current run has kept.

What it is not

  • It is not a budgeting app: it records your answer for each day and never connects to a bank.
  • It is not a treatment for compulsive buying; it is a self-tracking tool.

What it does

Every night

  • One question each night, answered No or Yes in the app or with one tap on the Home Screen widget; a kept day can also be marked from Control Center on iOS 18 or later, or by telling Siri “No spend day in NoBuy”.
  • Kept days fill a dial that counts toward the next milestone: 7, 14, 30, 60, 100 days and beyond.
  • Rent, bills, transport and groceries never break a streak: you mark the day “essentials only”.
  • A wrong answer can be edited or deleted, and the evening reminder knows when you have already answered.

When the urge hits

  • The urge timer runs ten minutes on a dial, started from the app or from Control Center on iOS 18 or later.
  • The impulse checklist asks five questions before you buy, such as “Will I remember wanting it in a month?”, and you can add your own, remove or reorder them.
  • The waiting list holds a thing for 24, 48 or 72 hours, with a reminder, before you decide.

Your record

  • Your streak, your best streak and the month so far, with the calendar and the last 90 days.
  • An estimate of what your current run has kept, from a typical spend day you enter, in your own currency; the total over every kept day is in Stats with NoBuy Pro.
  • 12 achievements, from your first day to 365, and a milestone card in story or square size to share or save.
  • Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, Control Center buttons on iOS 18 or later, and Siri: “No spend day in NoBuy” and “Check my streak in NoBuy”.
  • CSV export of every day on record, and erase at any time.

With NoBuy Pro

  • Every day on record back to the first, and the analysis: monthly trend, weekday pattern, streak history chart, day breakdown and no-spend rate.
  • Challenges of 14, 30, 60 or 100 days, or a length of your own up to 365 days.

How it works

  • A day you answer No keeps your streak, and so does a day you mark “essentials only”.
  • A streak freeze protects your streak for a day: one a month is included, and NoBuy Pro makes them unlimited.
  • The milestones are 7, 14, 30, 60 and 100 days and beyond, and the achievements run to 365 days.

Privacy

No bank connection, no account, no ads and no analytics. Everything you log stays on your device. The App Store privacy label says Data Not Collected.

Questions

Is there an app for a no-spend challenge?

Yes. NoBuy: No Spend Day Tracker asks one question each night, counts your kept days on a dial toward milestones of 7, 14, 30, 60 and 100 days, and includes a 7-day challenge without NoBuy Pro.

Is there an app with a waiting period before buying something?

Yes. NoBuy’s waiting list holds a thing for 24, 48 or 72 hours with a reminder, then you decide. Without NoBuy Pro you can add up to 3 things at once from the list itself; NoBuy Pro opens the list.

Does it have an urge timer for impulse spending?

Yes. The urge timer runs ten minutes on a dial, started from the app or from Control Center on iOS 18 or later. If you still want the thing afterward, you can put it on the waiting list.

Do rent, bills and groceries break my streak?

No. Mark the day “essentials only” and it keeps your streak; rent and bills, groceries and transport are built in as essentials.

Can I track a no-buy year?

Yes. The achievements run from your first day to 365, and a streak freeze protects your streak for a day: one a month is included, unlimited with NoBuy Pro. Seeing every day back to the first, and challenges of your own length up to 365 days, need NoBuy Pro.

Does NoBuy connect to my bank?

No. There is no bank connection and no account: you answer one question a day, and everything stays on your device.

Do I need NoBuy Pro?

No. The nightly question, your streak, the calendar and the last 90 days, the urge timer, the impulse checklist, the waiting list, every widget and CSV export work without NoBuy Pro. NoBuy Pro adds your full history, the analysis, longer challenges, unlimited streak freezes and an open waiting list.
