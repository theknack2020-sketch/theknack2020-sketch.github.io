Ethiopian Bible: Lost Books

Version 2.1.1 · November 13, 2026 · from TheKnack

Ethiopian Bible: Lost Books is an offline English Bible reader for iPhone and iPad, made for readers of the King James Bible who also want books of the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo canon such as Enoch, Jubilees and Meqabyan. The King James Bible (66 books) and the complete Book of Enoch can be read without the Full Library, which opens Jubilees, Jasher, Meqabyan and the 14 books of the KJV Apocrypha. Its Canon Map shows which of the 81 books of the Ethiopian canon are in the app in English and which are not in the app in English yet.

Who it is for

  • Readers who want the Book of Enoch, Jubilees, Jasher or Meqabyan in English, next to the King James Bible.
  • Readers of the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo canon who read in English and want to see which of its 81 books are in the app in English.
  • Readers of the King James Version who want the 1611 translators’ own margin notes.
  • Anyone who wants a Bible reader with no ads, no account and no need for an internet connection.

What it is not

  • It is not a complete 81-book or 88-book Ethiopian Bible in English: 9 of the 81 books are not in the app in English, and 3 are there only in part (1 Meqabyan; Psalms without Psalm 151; Jeremiah without 4 Baruch).
  • It is not a Ge’ez or Amharic Bible: the texts are English translations.
  • It is not a modern study translation: most texts are older English editions (the 1769 King James text, R. H. Charles’s translations of 1913 and 1917, the 1840 Jasher), and Meqabyan comes from a Wikisource community translation.

What it does

Read

  • The King James Bible, Old and New Testaments, 66 books, in the 1769 Oxford text.
  • The Book of Enoch (1 Enoch) in R. H. Charles’s 1917 translation, all 108 chapters.
  • With the Full Library: the Book of Jubilees in R. H. Charles’s 1913 translation with his chapter summaries, and the Book of Jasher in its 1840 English edition.
  • With the Full Library: 2 and 3 Meqabyan complete, and 7 of the 36 chapters of 1 Meqabyan.
  • With the Full Library: 14 books of the KJV Apocrypha, among them Tobit, Judith, Wisdom, Sirach, Baruch, 1 and 2 Maccabees and 1 and 2 Esdras.
  • The first chapter of every Full Library book can be read without it, so the four one-chapter books (the Song of the Three Holy Children, Susanna, Bel and the Dragon, and the Prayer of Manasseh) can be read in full.

Study

  • 6,959 margin notes by the 1611 King James translators sit beside the Old Testament verses they explain, and the translators’ preface is included in full.
  • An introduction to every book, and 44,104 cross-references, 44,080 of them from openbible.info; 10 of them lead into Full Library chapters.
  • The Canon Map draws the 66-book, the 81-book Ethiopian and the “88 books” lists as rings, with a source card for every book.
  • Search ranked by relevance, or inside a single book, plus highlights, bookmarks and your own notes.

Plans and sharing

  • The reading plan “Enoch in 7 Days” is included; plans for Jubilees, Meqabyan and a Lost Books sampler come with the Full Library.
  • The Plans tab opens the Gospel chapter behind four feasts and fasts of the Ethiopian year: Genna (Luke 2), Timkat (Matthew 3), Abiy Tsom, the Great Fast (Matthew 4), and Fasika (Matthew 28).
  • Any verse can be shared as a card with its source printed on it.
  • With the Full Library, highlights and notes export as Markdown.

Reading comfort

  • Paper, Sepia, Dark and a true-black Night theme, or Match System to follow the device’s appearance.
  • EB Garamond type, Ge’ez chapter numerals, adjustable size and line spacing, and full Dynamic Type.
  • Continue reading from Siri, Shortcuts and Spotlight.

How it works

  • The canon is counted book by book. The Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church keeps 81 books; 72 of them are in the app in English, 3 of those in part.
  • For some of the other 9 books no full English translation has ever been published; for others an older English translation exists but is not in the app yet.
  • Every book has a source card with its translator, year, source and license, and the full list of sources is in the app’s settings.
  • The sources are the King James Version, 1769 Oxford text (ebible.org); R. H. Charles’s Enoch (1917) and Jubilees (1913); the 1840 Book of Jasher; the Wikisource community translations of 1–3 Meqabyan (CC BY-SA 4.0); and cross-references from openbible.info (CC BY), based on the Treasury of Scripture Knowledge.

Privacy

No ads, no analytics and no account. The app never sends your notes and highlights anywhere; apart from your own device backups, they leave your device only if you export or share them yourself. The whole library is on the device, so the app reads without a connection. The App Store privacy label says Data Not Collected.

Questions

Is there an Ethiopian Bible app in English?

Yes. Ethiopian Bible: Lost Books is in English. It has 72 of the 81 books of the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo canon in English, 3 of them in part, and its Canon Map shows which books are not in the app in English yet.

Can I read Meqabyan in English?

Yes, with the Full Library: 2 and 3 Meqabyan complete and 7 of the 36 chapters of 1 Meqabyan, from the Wikisource community translations (CC BY-SA 4.0). The first chapter of each can be read without the Full Library.

Does it have the Book of Enoch in R. H. Charles’s translation?

Yes. All 108 chapters of 1 Enoch in R. H. Charles’s 1917 translation are included without the Full Library, with a 7-day reading plan.

Does it include Jubilees and the Book of Jasher?

Yes, both are in the Full Library: Jubilees in R. H. Charles’s 1913 translation with his chapter summaries, and Jasher in its 1840 English edition. The first chapter of each is included.

Does it have the 1611 King James translators’ margin notes?

Yes. 6,959 margin notes by the 1611 translators sit beside the Old Testament verses they explain, and their preface is included in full, without the Full Library.

Is it a King James Bible with the Apocrypha?

Yes. The 66 books of the King James Bible are included, and the Full Library adds 14 books of the KJV Apocrypha, among them Tobit, Judith, Wisdom, Sirach, Baruch, 1 and 2 Maccabees and 1 and 2 Esdras.

Is it the complete 88-book Ethiopian Bible?

No. Some books of the Ethiopian canon have never been published in full in English, so the app does not claim 88 books; the Canon Map shows which books are in it, which are partial and which are missing.

Do I need the Full Library or an internet connection?

No. The King James Bible, Enoch, the margin notes, the Canon Map and search need no Full Library, and the whole library is on the device, so it works offline.
