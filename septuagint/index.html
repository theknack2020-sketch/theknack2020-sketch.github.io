Septuagint

The Orthodox Old Testament

The Greek Old Testament in English: the Old Testament the New Testament writers quoted, and the Old Testament of the Eastern Orthodox Church. 52 books, complete, in Sir Lancelot Brenton's 1851 translation, with his Greek text beside it.

Its signature is the Apostles' Map: more than 1,600 places where the New Testament quotes or alludes to the Old, each linked to the verse it came from. A quotation is marked with the text its wording follows: the Greek, the Hebrew, or neither exactly.

The English text, search and the whole map are free. The app collects nothing, sends nothing, and requests no permissions at all.
