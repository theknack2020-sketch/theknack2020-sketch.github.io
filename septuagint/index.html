Septuagint: Apostles' Bible

The Orthodox Old Testament

The Greek Old Testament in English — the Old Testament the New Testament writers actually quoted, and the canonical Old Testament of the Eastern Orthodox Church. Fifty-one books, complete, in Sir Lancelot Brenton's 1851 translation.

Its signature is the Apostles' Map: every New Testament quotation of the Old, linked to the verse it came from and marked with which of the two ancient text traditions the writer followed — the Greek, the Hebrew, or neither exactly.

Scripture, search and export are free forever. The app collects nothing, sends nothing, and requests no permissions at all.
