Why is Psalm 23 numbered 22 here?

The Greek and Hebrew traditions divide the same 150 psalms differently in four places, which shifts every number in between by one. The App always shows both numbers, and searching for a psalm in either numbering finds it. There is a second, smaller shift as well: the Greek counts a psalm's title as verse 1 and the Hebrew does not, so verse numbers can differ by one too.

What is the difference between a quotation and an allusion?

A quotation is a place where a New Testament writer reproduces the wording of an Old Testament passage. An allusion points back at a passage without reproducing it. The Apostles' Map lists both and says which is which.

What do the three marks on a quotation mean?

When a New Testament writer quotes the Old Testament, he may follow the Greek text, the Hebrew text, or neither exactly. The mark says which. "Neither exactly" is an ordinary and common answer: often the writer was quoting from memory, paraphrasing, or drawing on both.

Why does Proverbs 30 look empty?

The Greek orders Proverbs differently from the Hebrew. The material printed as chapter 30 in Hebrew-order Bibles appears in chapter 24 here, and the App says so on that page.

Which books are included?

All 52 books of this edition, including the deuterocanonical books read in the Orthodox and Catholic churches, and Psalm 151, which the Hebrew Bible does not have.

I had notes and bookmarks in version 1. Where are they?

They are kept. Version 2 corrected the numbering of some chapters and verses, and your marks moved with the text they were made on: a mark on Nehemiah, for example, is now listed under 2 Esdras, which is where this edition prints Nehemiah. A copy of your version 1 data was also kept on your device before anything was changed.

Managing your subscription

Septuagint Pro is an auto-renewing subscription available monthly or annually. Manage or cancel it in Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions on your device. To use a subscription on another of your devices, use Restore Purchases in the App's Settings.
