Content and sources

The App contains public-domain texts: the Septuagint in Sir Lancelot Charles Lee Brenton's English translation (1851) and its Greek text, obtained from eBible.org; the King James Version (1769 text) used for New Testament quotation text; and the Textus Receptus Greek New Testament. Quotation pairings derive in part from openbible.info cross-reference data, used under the Creative Commons Attribution licence. The typeface Literata is used under the SIL Open Font Licence 1.1.

The Apostles' Map's tradition badges are produced by our own measurement of these texts, with a small number of entries decided by hand; they are offered as scholarship, not as doctrine.
