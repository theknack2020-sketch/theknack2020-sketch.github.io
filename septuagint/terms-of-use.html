What is free

These are free of charge, with no time limit:

  • All 52 books of the Greek Old Testament in English, complete.
  • Full-text search across every book.
  • The whole Apostles' Map, in both directions: from a New Testament verse to its source, and from an Old Testament verse to every passage that draws on it.
  • The Greek and Hebrew psalm numbers, side by side.
  • The Greek text beside the English in chapter 1 of every book, and for the first 3 verses of every other chapter.
  • The first 3 rows of every map insight.
  • One reading plan in full, and the first 7 days of every other plan.
  • A short introduction to every book.
  • 25 bookmarks, 25 highlights and 25 notes, and export of everything you write.

The English text is never placed behind a purchase.

Content and sources

The App contains public-domain texts: the Septuagint in Sir Lancelot Charles Lee Brenton's English translation (1851) and the Greek Septuagint he compiled, both obtained from eBible.org; and the King James Version (the 1769 text), used for the New Testament side of the Apostles' Map. In the United Kingdom the Crown holds rights in the King James Version, administered by Cambridge University Press. The links of the Apostles' Map derive from openbible.info cross-reference data, used under the Creative Commons Attribution licence, selected and measured for the App against the Greek New Testament (Textus Receptus). The typeface Literata is used under the SIL Open Font Licence 1.1.

Whether a link is called a quotation or an allusion, and whether a quotation is marked as following the Greek, the Hebrew or neither, comes from our own comparison of these texts, with a number of entries decided by hand. The book introductions are our own writing. Both are offered as an aid to reading, not as doctrine.
