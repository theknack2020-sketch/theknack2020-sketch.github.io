Privacy Policy

Septuagint: Apostles' Bible · Last updated 10 August 2026

The short version

The App collects nothing. It sends nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no network connection of any kind beyond Apple's own purchase flow. Nothing you read, search for or write ever leaves your device.

Data we collect

None. We operate no server and receive no information about you or your use of the App. Its App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected".

Data stored on your device

Your bookmarks, highlights, notes and reading position are stored locally on your device by the App. They are not transmitted anywhere and we have no access to them. Deleting the App deletes them.

Permissions

The App requests no permissions at all — no camera, no photo library, no notifications, no location, no contacts, no microphone.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple may provide us with anonymous, aggregated sales statistics that cannot identify you.

Children

The App is suitable for general audiences and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the revised version will appear on this page with a new date. Since we collect nothing, any change is likely to concern wording rather than practice.
