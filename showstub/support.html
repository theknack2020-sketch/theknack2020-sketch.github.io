Bringing back the nights you already lived

Showstub reads your photo library on your phone, finds the evenings that look like events — bursts of photos taken close together after 17:00 — and proposes them on the roll. You confirm what was real and name the place; nothing is kept until you tear the stub.

  • It needs full library access to find nights. With selected photos only the pictures you pick can become stubs; with no access you add nights yourself. The app prints all three outcomes before it asks.
  • Photos are read for their dates, times and (when present) locations only. No image content is analysed, and nothing is uploaded.
  • A photo with no location still counts — the app says "no place on it" and lets you name the venue when you keep the night.
  • Dismissed a night by mistake? Tap Undo on the bar that appears. Dismissed nights are never proposed again automatically; Settings → Dismissed nights → Propose them again brings them back.

Scanning a ticket

Tap Scan ticket in the bar at the bottom of the book and point the camera at the printed side of a ticket. Showstub reads the text on the device and proposes the act, venue, date, seat and price in an editable form. It reads plain text, not barcodes or QR codes. If the print is faint, Type it instead is one tap away and nothing is lost.

The free tier and Showstub Pro

Every event you remember is free, with no cap. Showstub Pro adds depth: all stub stocks, every year's book open in one continuous view, the year recap rendered as a shareable card, Home and Lock Screen widgets, and full-resolution export. Earlier years are never locked away — on the free tier they sit one tap behind the current year and remain fully editable.

Managing or cancelling a subscription

Subscriptions are handled by Apple, not by us: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Showstub. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Refund requests go to Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Restoring a purchase

Open the gear at the top left of the book → Showstub Pro → Restore. If it says nothing was found, the subscription is probably on a different Apple Account — sign in with that one and try again.

Fixing a mistake

Every stub can be edited or deleted — tap it, then Edit. Deleting shows an Undo bar for a few seconds. A mistyped entry is never permanent.

Widgets

Add the Showstub widget from the Home Screen or Lock Screen widget gallery. It shows how many nights you have kept, the most recent stub, and any night from this day in an earlier year. Widgets are a Pro depth; a locked widget opens the Pro screen when tapped.

Where your data lives

On your iPhone, and nowhere else. There is no account, no cloud sync in this version, and no server. Because of that, keep your encrypted device backup switched on. Details in the Privacy Policy.
