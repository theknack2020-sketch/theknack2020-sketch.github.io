The short version:

Showstub keeps the shows, matches, films and journeys you have already been to, as perforated ticket stubs filed by year. Every event you remember stays free, with no cap on how many you keep. Showstub Pro is an auto-renewing subscription that adds depth — stub stocks, every year's book open at once, the year recap card, widgets and full-resolution export. Your collection lives on your device; we never receive it.

2. What the free version includes

Without paying anything, Showstub gives you:

  • Unlimited events. The number of stubs you keep is never capped, on any tier.
  • All three ways in — photograph a ticket, pick a photo, or type a night in.
  • The photo-library pass that finds the evenings that look like events and proposes them for you to confirm.

  • Statistics — how many nights, how many cities, the act you have seen most, what a year cost.
  • Editing and deleting every record you create. A mistyped entry is never permanent.
  • Earlier years remain open, readable, editable and deletable. Nothing you kept is ever locked away.
