Privacy Policy

Effective: September 4, 2026 · Last updated: September 4, 2026

The short version:

Your collection stays on your device. Every stub, note, seat, price and photo you keep in Showstub is stored only on your iPhone using Apple's SwiftData, with iCloud sync switched off in this version. Your photo library and your camera are read on the phone to propose nights and read tickets — nothing from either is ever uploaded. There is no account and no login. Showstub has no analytics and no tracking of any kind, and we never show ads.

1. Who we are

Showstub ("the app", "we", "us") is developed and distributed by TheKnack. Questions or concerns: theknack2020@gmail.com.

2. Your collection stays on your device

Showstub is a personal archive of events you have already attended. Everything you record is stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's SwiftData, and it is kept there only:

  • Events — the act, team, film or journey; its category; its date and time
  • Places — venue, city, country
  • Seats — block, row, seat
  • What it cost, who you were with, and your own note
  • References to the photos you attach (the photos themselves stay in your Photos library)
  • Nights you dismissed from the proposal roll, so they are not proposed again

We do not upload, mirror, sync or read any of this. There is no Showstub server. This version keeps CloudKit sync switched off, so your collection is not placed in iCloud by the app. The only network activity is Apple's own App Store purchase check.

3. Your photo library

Showstub's signature feature reads your photo library on your device to find evenings that look like events — clusters of photos taken close together in the evening — and proposes them for you to confirm. Before asking, the app prints all three outcomes so you can decide with the facts:

  • Full library: Showstub reads photo dates, times and (if present) locations on the device, groups them, and proposes nights. It never uploads a photo, a date or a location, and it never reads image content.
  • Selected photos: only the photos you hand-pick can become stubs; nights cannot be found, because the library cannot be read. The app says so plainly.
  • No access: you add nights yourself. Everything else works.

The permission request appears only after the app has explained what it does with the library, never on first launch, and the app respects whatever you decide. Changing your mind later is done in iOS Settings.

4. The camera

Showstub can use the camera for one job: reading the printed text on a ticket you photograph — act, venue, date, seat, price — using Apple's on-device text recognition. The picture stays on your phone. Nothing captured by the camera is stored by the app or sent anywhere; what it reads becomes a proposal in an editable form that you confirm. The camera is never used for anything else, and the app works fully without it.

5. Analytics: none

Showstub contains no analytics or telemetry of any kind. There is no third-party analytics SDK, no usage tracking, and no device identifier collection — not even an anonymous "app opened" counter ever leaves your phone.

This is why Showstub's App Store privacy label reads "Data Not Collected". Any sense of how the app is doing comes only from Apple's own aggregate App Store Connect figures, which never reach into your device.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not require an account, sign-up or login.
  • We do not store your stubs, notes or photos on any server — there is no Showstub server.
  • We do not upload or analyse your photos; the library pass runs on the device and reads only dates, times and locations.
  • We do not place your collection in iCloud; this version has CloudKit sync switched off.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers, and we never show ads.
  • We do not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt — there is nothing to track.
  • We do not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs — only Apple's own frameworks (SwiftUI, SwiftData, PhotoKit, VisionKit, StoreKit, WidgetKit).

7. Widgets

If you add a Showstub widget, the app writes a small snapshot of your collection (counts, the most recent stub, and anniversaries) to a storage area shared with the widget on your own device. It never leaves the phone.

8. App Store purchases

If you subscribe to Showstub Pro (monthly or yearly), Apple processes the transaction. We receive from Apple only a receipt confirming your entitlement status; we do not receive your payment information. Apple's privacy policy governs payment data: apple.com/legal/privacy.

9. Children

Showstub is a personal record-keeping tool and is not directed to children. Because the app collects nothing from anyone, there is no children's data for us to hold.

10. Keeping, changing and deleting your records

  • Edit: every stub you create can be corrected in the app, in every year. A mistyped entry is never a permanent error.
  • Delete: every stub can be removed. A deletion can be undone for a few seconds right afterwards; after that it is gone.
  • Delete everything: deleting the app removes all of its local data from your device. Since Showstub keeps nothing in iCloud and nothing on a server, that is the end of it.
  • Backups: copies may persist inside encrypted device or iCloud device backups you keep, which are managed by Apple, until those backups are deleted or rotate out.

We retain no server-side copy of your content, because we never receive it.

11. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA and other regions)

Because we hold no personal data about you on any server, most data-subject requests resolve on your own device:

  • Showstub collects nothing about you, so there is no personal data held by us to access, correct or erase.
  • We do not "sell" or "share" personal information as those terms are defined under the CCPA/CPRA.
  • EU/EEA and UK residents retain all rights under the GDPR/UK GDPR, including access, rectification, erasure, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. California residents retain all rights under the CCPA/CPRA.
  • To exercise any right, or if you have questions, email theknack2020@gmail.com.

12. Changes to this policy

If we ever change what the app does with data, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above before the change ships. If a future version adds optional iCloud sync, that version's policy will describe it and the app will ask first.
