Selenora — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

The short version

Selenora collects nothing. There is no analytics SDK, no tracking, no advertising identifier and no account. Everything the app shows is computed on your device from the date and a place.

Location

If you allow it, Selenora reads your location once while the app is open, to compute moonrise for where you are. The coordinates are stored on your device only and are never sent anywhere. You can decline and pick a city from the list built into the app instead; every feature works the same way.

What stays on your device

Your saved places and your preferences are stored locally on your iPhone and, if you use it, shared with your Apple Watch through Apple's own device-to-device channel. Nothing is stored on our side, because there is no our side.

Network use

Selenora makes no network requests of its own. It has no server and nothing to fetch: the moon's position, phases, rise and set times and eclipses are calculated inside the app. The only network traffic is Apple's own purchase and subscription flow, which Apple handles.

Notifications

Alerts are scheduled on your device from the same calculations. Turning them off in iOS Settings changes nothing else about the app.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. Selenora receives from Apple only whether a subscription is active. We never see your payment details.

Children

Selenora is rated 4+ and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date above changes with it.
