Questions we get

Why is the rim's north at the top? The rim is your horizon, drawn from above, so a tick at ENE means the moon comes up east-northeast. Point the phone north and the tick points at the horizon where it rises.

Why does the time differ from another app? Times depend on the exact coordinates and time zone. Selenora uses the place you chose; a city centre and its airport can differ by a minute or two. Refraction is modelled with the standard value; mountains and buildings are not.

The moon isn't drawn the way I see it. The face shows the lit fraction with north at the top of the sky. Near the horizon the real moon appears rotated; south of the equator the lit side is mirrored, which Selenora does automatically for southern places.

What is a supermoon here? A full moon within the closest tenth of its perigee–apogee range for that orbit — the same rule most published lists use.

What is free and what is Pro? Tonight, the calendar, one saved place, one small Home Screen widget, the full-moon alert and events to 90 nights are free. Pro adds every other widget size and surface, Apple Watch complications, unlimited places, the other alerts and events beyond 90 nights.

How do I cancel? iOS Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions. Cancelling keeps Pro until the end of the paid period; the free tier continues afterwards.

The Watch shows a different place. The wrist follows the phone's default place. Open Selenora on the phone once after changing places; the watch updates through Apple's device-to-device channel.
