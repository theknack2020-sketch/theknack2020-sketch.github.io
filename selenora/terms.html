Last updated: 8 September 2026

What Selenora is

Selenora shows the moon for a place and a night: rise and set times and bearings, phase and illumination, distance, and upcoming full moons, new moons, supermoons, blue moons and lunar eclipses. Everything is computed on your device from published astronomical algorithms.

What is free

Tonight, the calendar, one saved place, one small Home Screen widget, the full-moon notification and events for the next 90 nights are free, permanently, with no account.

Selenora Pro

Selenora Pro is an auto-renewable subscription sold through Apple: one monthly plan and one yearly plan, each with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. It adds every other widget size and surface (medium, large, Lock Screen, StandBy), Apple Watch complications, unlimited saved places, the moonrise, new-moon, supermoon and eclipse alerts, and events beyond 90 nights.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase, or at the end of the free trial if one applies. The subscription renews automatically unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends. You can manage or cancel it in iOS Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions. Prices are shown in the app in your local currency, from the App Store.

Accuracy

Times are computed to the minute for the coordinates and time zone of the place you choose. Refraction, elevation, horizon obstructions and weather are not modelled; treat the figures as a guide to the sky, not as navigation, tidal, religious or legal timing. Astronomy only: Selenora gives no astrological, medical or lifestyle advice.

Your data

Saved places and preferences are stored on your device. See the Privacy Policy.
