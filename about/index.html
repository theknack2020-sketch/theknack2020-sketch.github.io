About TheKnack

TheKnack is the name behind 32 apps for iPhone and iPad on the App Store. Each app’s App Store page names TheKnack in its copyright line, and all of the apps are listed together on one App Store developer page. The earliest of these apps, dB Meter: Decibel Sound Level, has been on the App Store since March 2026.

See all apps on the App Store

Contact

Questions about any app: support@theknackapps.com. Each app page also links to that app’s privacy policy and terms of use.

How the app pages are written

The five app pages linked from the home page each describe the version that is live on the App Store: Ethiopian Bible 2.1.1, Septuagint 2.0.0, NoBuy 2.1.1, dB Meter 3.0.0 and FanWake 3.1.0. Each page says what is included with the download and what the optional upgrade adds. The pages show no ratings, no testimonials and no prices: the price in your currency is shown in the app and on the App Store before you confirm.
