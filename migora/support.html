Support

Migora is a private, account-free migraine tracker for iPhone and iPad. Here are the answers to the things people ask most. If yours isn't here, email us — we read everything.

Migora is a tracking tool, not a medical device. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a healthcare professional.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account?

No. There's no account with us, no sign-up, and no login. You open the app and start tracking. Your log stays on your device — there's nothing to create and nothing to sign in to.

Where is my data stored? Is it private?

Everything you log — attacks, symptoms, medications, triggers, check-ins, questionnaire answers, and pressure history — stays on your device. Nothing is ever sent to a TheKnack server — there isn't one. Migora has no analytics at all, and your health data is never sold or shared.

What happens when I get a new phone?

Migora stores your log on the device where you created it — it isn't synced to an account or a server. The simplest way to bring it to a new phone is to restore the new device from your old device's backup (iCloud Backup or an encrypted computer backup), which includes Migora's data. You can also use the CSV export (Settings → Export All Data as CSV) to keep a copy of your records.

Does Migora connect to Apple Health?

Not in this version. Migora doesn't read from or write to Apple Health — everything you track is entered inside the app and stays in Migora. If Apple Health support is added later, it will be optional and will ask for your permission first.

How do pressure features and weather work?

Migora records pressure from your device's built-in barometer while the app is open, and fetches hourly pressure for your area from Apple's WeatherKit. The weather request needs an approximate location, which is sent to Apple, never to us. Location is optional: without it, the barometer and your diary still work. Today's pressure and the past 48 hours, with your attacks marked, are included for everyone. The next 48 hours of pressure and pressure-drop alerts are part of Migora Pro. Pressure information is descriptive only — Migora does not predict attacks.

What does Migora Pro unlock?

Without Pro you get unlimited attack logging, Attack Mode, your full calendar and history, daily check-ins, reminders, the widget, today's pressure and the past 48 hours, and CSV export — none of it is ever locked. Migora Pro adds the next 48 hours of pressure, pressure-drop alerts, the Insights analysis (your attacks laid over weeks of pressure, trigger and medication patterns) and the doctor report PDF.

How much does Migora Pro cost?

Migora Pro is a monthly or a yearly subscription. Your local price is shown in the app before you buy. Where offered, the yearly plan starts with a one-week free trial for new subscribers; then it renews yearly until you cancel.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. Open iPhone Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Migora → Cancel Subscription. You keep Pro until the end of the current billing period. If you are on a free trial and do not want to be charged, cancel at least 24 hours before it ends.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles refunds. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and select the Migora purchase. If Apple declines, email us and we'll see what we can do.

I bought Pro but it isn't showing / I'm on a new device

In Migora, open the Migora Pro screen (Settings → Upgrade to Migora Pro) and tap Restore. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy. Restore re-applies your purchase from Apple's records.

Is a one-time Pro unlock still valid?

Yes. A one-time Pro unlock stays valid, with nothing more to pay. If it isn't showing, tap Restore on the Migora Pro screen.

How do I share my history with my doctor?

Open Insights → Doctor Report. With Migora Pro you get a PDF of your log covering several months, with a calendar heat map, how often and how long your attacks lasted, acute-medication days and the triggers you tagged most. You can print or share it. It's a summary of your own records to bring to an appointment, not a clinical assessment.

How do reminders and alerts work?

Migora can send a daily check-in nudge, a gentle post-attack follow-up, (with Pro) a pressure-change alert, and, if you haven't opened Migora for a week, at most two short notes a week apart that your log is there when you need it. They're scheduled locally on your device, so they work without any push server, and they're always gentle and never pushy. Enable notifications when Migora asks, or later in iPhone Settings → Migora → Notifications.

How do I export or delete my data?

You can export your log as CSV at any time from Settings → Export All Data as CSV; it is always included. To erase everything on your device, use Settings → Delete All Data inside Migora, or delete the app. If you keep device backups, remember a copy may remain in your Apple-managed backup until it's deleted or rotates out.

Will Migora support other languages?

Migora is in English for now. We want the experience to feel right in one language before expanding. Additional languages may come in future updates.

Troubleshooting

My reminders aren't arriving

Open iPhone Settings → Migora → Notifications and make sure they're allowed. Then confirm you've set a reminder time inside Migora. If you also have Focus or Do Not Disturb on, check that Migora is allowed to notify you.

Pressure or weather isn't loading

Pressure features use Apple WeatherKit, which needs a network connection and location access. Check iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Migora, and make sure you're online. Weather can take a moment to refresh after you move to a new area.

My data is missing

Your data lives on the device where you created it. If you recently switched to a new device, the data remains on the old one unless you restored the new device from the old device's backup. Restore from that backup, or use the CSV export from the old device to keep a copy of your records.
